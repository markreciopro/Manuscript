The Five-Module Curriculum & Capstone

A progressive ascension from raw PMS records to executive boardroom recommendations.

1

Reading the Landscape

Master data architecture, demand segmentation (Leisure, Corporate, Group, Wholesale), and the four strict criteria required for a legitimate comparable set.

2

The Math of a Room Night

Unpack Occupancy, Average Daily Rate (ADR), and Revenue Per Available Room (RevPAR) while avoiding the traps of metrics moving in opposite directions.

Benchmarking the Property

5

Forecasting & Market Direction

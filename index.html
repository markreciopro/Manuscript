The Core Curriculum Framework

A progressive ascension from raw PMS records to executive boardroom recommendations.

1

Reading the Landscape

Master data architecture, demand segmentation (Leisure, Corporate, Group, Wholesale), and rigorous comparable set criteria.

2

The Math of a Room Night

Unpack Occupancy, Average Daily Rate (ADR), and RevPAR while avoiding the traps of metrics moving in opposite directions.

3

Benchmarking the Property

Calculate MPI, ARI, and RGI fair-share indexes and uncover the hidden "index mirage" where monthly averages conceal weaknesses.

4

Labour, Cost & Profit

Bridge revenue to the bottom line using TrevPAR, GOPPAR, flow-through percentages, and Minutes Per Occupied Room (MPOR).

5

Forecasting & Market Direction

Evaluate room supply pipelines, market absorption, demand generators, and build robust three-scenario forecasts.

★

Capstone: The Boardroom

Synthesize all modules into a single, defensible ownership recommendation that survives rigorous executive cross-examination.
